# zupmxode 0.0.0.9000

* Initial CRAN submission.

* `pmx_ode_tier()` reports and sets the tier a model runs on: native code
  generated by MIR, or MIR's interpreter.

* `pmx_ode_model()` compiles a model written in the subset of rxode2's
  model language described in its documentation, including `linCmt()`.

* `pmx_ode_events()` builds a NONMEM-style event table of doses and
  observation times.

* `pmx_ode_solve()` solves closed-form models (`linCmt()`) for one or more
  subjects, exactly, with time-varying covariates, resets and steady state.

* `pmx_ode_solve()` solves models with no closed form, with LSODA (from the
  bundled liblsoda) or Dormand-Prince 5(4).

* Linear models are solved exactly between events by the matrix
  exponential.

* `pmx_ode_method()` reports and sets a model's solver; `pmx_ode_info()`
  says what a model is, which solver it takes and why, its tier and how
  long it took to compile.

* Subjects are solved in parallel with OpenMP; `pmx_ode_threads()` sets how
  many threads, one by default. Results are the same, bit for bit, at any
  thread count.

* `pmx_ode_tier()` keeps a model's compartments, outputs and solver when it
  makes the copy on another tier.

* `pmx_ode_sim()` simulates a population: etas drawn from `omega`, and
  residual error from `add()`, `prop()`, `add() + prop()` or `lnorm()`,
  every draw made with R's random number generator, so `set.seed()` makes
  a simulation reproducible at any thread count.

* `pmx_ode_subjects()` prepares subjects once and `pmx_ode_subject_eval()`
  evaluates one at a theta and eta, giving its predictions (the doubles
  `pmx_ode_solve()` gives) and residual variances, as an estimator needs.
  The same call is in C, `zupmxode_subject_eval()`, through the header
  `inst/include/zupmxode.h`, re-entrant and allocation-free, for packages
  that link to zupmxode.

* `pmx_ode_solve()` reads NONMEM's `MDV` when a table has no `EVID`: a
  record with no dose and `MDV` 1 marks a time rather than being an
  observation.
